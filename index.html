A QUICK GUIDE

Make it yours.

  1. Arrange your boards. Drag each board by its Move handle. Bring their edges close to snap them together.
  2. Choose a board. Select either board on the right to change its size or color.
  3. Add accessories. Drag from the catalog onto either board or use the plus buttons.
  4. Fine-tune your setup. Drag items to move them between boards. Select one to rotate, duplicate, or remove it.
  5. Keep your idea. Your layout saves in this browser. Download an image or copy a link to share it.
