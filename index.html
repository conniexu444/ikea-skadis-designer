Plan it before you drill.

SKÅDIS Studio lays out real SKÅDIS hole grids so you can see exactly what fits.

  1. Arrange your boards. Drag a board by its handle; edges snap together when they get close.
  2. Mount them. Put wall mounts on the holes that will hold each board to the wall.
  3. Add accessories. Drag pieces onto the board or press +. Pieces snap to holes, and each hole holds one thing.
  4. Fine-tune. Select a piece to rotate, copy, or remove it. Arrow keys move it hole by hole.
  5. Keep it. Your layout saves in this browser. Copy a link or download a PNG to share it.
